================================================================================
Task 68: Clicking a region row in the right-hand list brings that region to
         the centre of the visible image area
================================================================================

This order is Task 68 of the commander's local ROADMAP (an approver's request
of 2026-10-10; not on GitHub; you do not need it: everything is in this order).

## 1. Purpose and background

During the human check the approver zooms in on a newspaper page and works
through the regions listed in the right-hand list. To look at a region, the
approver has to find it on the page and drag (Pan) or scroll the image until
it is in view. The approver asked (2026-10-10): when a row in the right-hand
list is clicked, the image should move so that this region is in the centre,
to save that moving.

Design (decided by the commander, checked on screen in a browser):

  - Clicking a region row (the row Container's on_click, i.e. make_active)
    calls a new method center_on_region(rid) BEFORE the existing code. It
    does so even when the row is already the active one (the approver may
    have panned away since).
  - center_on_region moves the image with the same offsets that Pan uses
    (self.offset_x / self.offset_y), and moves the image and the three
    overlay layers (highlight, rects, inline editor) by exactly the same
    amount, so the frames stay on the image. The zoom does not change.
  - The offset puts the centre of the region's bbox at the centre of the
    visible area (self.win_w x self.win_h). On an axis where the whole image
    already fits in the visible area (img_len * zoom <= view_len), the offset
    on that axis is 0 (nothing moves). Regions near the page edge are still
    centred (blank space outside the page may show, as with Pan today).
  - The two scrollables around the image (self.scrollable_image and its only
    child Row) are scrolled back to 0, because the offset is computed for
    scroll position 0. This is done only when the control is on a page.
  - Unknown region id, or a page whose image failed to load (ocr_error):
    do nothing.
  - It changes nothing else: no edits, no order, no bbox, no saving.
